Glazing for Controlled Environments

Where temperature, humidity or process conditions are tightly managed, glazing becomes part of the system. Our coatings reduce infrared heat transfer, keep surfaces clear and dissipate static, on a substrate that tolerates thermal shock.

  • IRR coatings to reduce radiant heat gain or loss through glazing
  • TCO coatings for transparent heated, anti-fog or shielded panels
  • ATO coatings for static control and infrared management
  • Tempered or annealed, 1/8" to 5/16" thickness

Typical Applications

  • Climate-controlled and cold-storage observation windows
  • Environmental chamber and cleanroom viewing panels
  • Oven, kiln and fireplace glazing
  • Specialty interior glazing with iridescent IRR finish

Maximum sheet size: 96" x 48" (2438 mm x 1219 mm). Thicknesses from 1/8" to 5/16". Larger and thicker than commercially coated soda-lime float can supply.
