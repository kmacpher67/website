Advanced Materials Engineering: Large Sheet Borosilicate & Quartz Glass

Commercially available coated soda-lime glass is limited in thickness and size, and fails under extreme thermal shock or in explosion-prone environments. We apply custom Infra-Red Reflecting (IRR), Transparent Conductive Oxide (TCO) and Antimony Tin Oxide (ATO) coatings to large sheets of borosilicate (BPS) and quartz glass so engineers can specify one panel instead of a tiled workaround.

Custom Coatings on Thermal Shock Resistant Glass Panels

Custom ATO Coated Glass

Antimony Tin Oxide coatings for infrared control and static-dissipative, conductive surfaces on borosilicate and quartz substrates.

IRR Coated Quartz Glass

Infra-Red Reflecting coatings that manage radiant heat on quartz and BPS for furnaces, optical paths and high-temperature enclosures.

TCO Glass Manufacturer

Transparent Conductive Oxide coatings for heating, EMI shielding, de-icing and sensing, applied to large-format sheets.

Thermal Shock Resistant Glass Panels & Explosion Proof Viewports

Borosilicate and quartz tolerate rapid temperature change that cracks coated soda-lime. Our coated panels are used as the glazing for viewports in thermal shock and explosion-prone environments. Pressure, impact and hazardous-area ratings are specific to each application, so we engineer to your stated requirements and verify them with you during the technical evaluation.
