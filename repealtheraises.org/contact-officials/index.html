Warren’s Declaration of Accountability

Public office is public service. Warren’s elected officials answer to the people. Find your council representative and contact Warren’s elected leadership respectfully about Draft 0883, elected-official compensation, and the voters’ decision.

Be firm, factual and respectful. Ask for a clear answer, record the response, and follow up when necessary.

About this directory

Names, public office phone numbers and public email addresses come from the City of Warren pages linked on each card, last read November 23, 2026. Residential addresses are intentionally left out. Terms of office are not published on those pages, so none are listed; check the Trumbull County Board of Elections. No source states that any number accepts text messages, so Text buttons say “Text if supported.” The Law Director has only the Law Department’s shared address published. Legal questions about Ohio Revised Code § 731.07 are open questions and challenges, not court findings.
