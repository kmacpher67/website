Laser Windows and Enclosure Glass

Laser systems concentrate heat and demand stable optics. Quartz and borosilicate handle thermal loading and shock, and our coatings control reflectance, infrared transmission and surface conductivity for the application.

  • IRR coatings to reflect unwanted infrared and protect enclosures
  • TCO coatings for transparent heating, EMI shielding and electrostatic control
  • ATO coatings for static dissipation and infrared management
  • Single-piece panels up to 96" x 48" (2438 x 1219 mm)

Typical Applications

  • Industrial and research laser enclosure windows
  • Medical and diagnostic equipment viewing panels
  • Furnace, vacuum and process-chamber windows
  • Transparent heated or shielded panels for lab instruments

Specify With Us

Wavelength, power density, operating temperature and cleanliness requirements drive the coating choice. We review these during the technical evaluation and confirm feasibility before quoting. Regulatory and laser-safety certifications remain the responsibility of the final system integrator.

Maximum sheet size: 96" x 48" (2438 mm x 1219 mm). Thicknesses from 1/8" to 5/16". Larger and thicker than commercially coated soda-lime float can supply.
